## ✨ What Is MirrorAct?

MirrorAct takes the picture from your iPhone or iPad and puts it on your computer screen in real time. Instead of a bare rectangle floating in a window, you get your device shown inside a clean, accurate frame — like a product photo, but live.

From there you can:

- Take a screenshot of exactly what your phone is showing, frame included.
- Record a video of your screen with smooth motion and clear detail.
- Swap the background behind the device to a color, a gradient, or your own image.
- Present your phone screen to an audience in a large, tidy layout.

Everything happens on your own computer. You connect your device with a cable or over AirPlay, and MirrorAct handles the rest.

The project is completely open source under the GPL-3.0 license, which means anyone can look at how it works, suggest improvements, or build on it.

## 📱 Connecting Your Device

MirrorAct supports two ways to connect, and both are simple.

**USB cable.** Plug your iPhone or iPad into your computer with its charging cable. On the device, a small message may appear asking whether you trust this computer. Tap Trust and enter your passcode if asked. MirrorAct will then pick up the screen automatically.

**AirPlay.** Make sure your device and your computer are on the same Wi-Fi network. Open Control Center on your iPhone or iPad, tap the Screen Mirroring button, and choose your computer from the list. The screen will appear in MirrorAct within a few seconds.

If you connect with a cable, the picture is usually sharper and smoother, which matters most when recording. AirPlay is more convenient when you want to move around freely.

## 🎬 Recording and Screenshots

To take a screenshot, connect your device, arrange the frame and background the way you like, and press the screenshot button. The image is saved straight away.

To record, press the record button. A small indicator shows that recording is running. Do whatever you want to show on your device, then press stop. Your video file is saved when you stop.

A few practical tips:

- Do a five-second test recording before an important session, so you know where the file ends up.
- Close other apps on your device if you want a clean, distraction-free capture.
- For the sharpest recording, use a cable rather than AirPlay.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**My device does not appear.** Unplug the cable and plug it back in, or turn AirPlay off and on again. Make sure you tapped Trust on the device if the prompt appeared.

**The picture is lagging.** Switch from AirPlay to a USB cable. Wireless mirroring depends on your network and can stutter.

**The recording looks blurry.** Record over a cable, close heavy apps on your computer, and avoid moving the window around while recording.

**The security warning keeps appearing.** That message is your computer being careful about downloaded software. Choose the option to open or allow the app, and it will not ask again.

**The app closes unexpectedly.** Quit it completely, then reopen it. If the problem continues, download a fresh copy from the link above and replace the old one.
